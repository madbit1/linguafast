## Accuracy and safety

Website phone and chat examples are **illustrations, not screenshots** or proof of translation correctness. The secure in-app updater has now been confirmed on the user’s Samsung through v36. The user still approves Android installation. Play Protect and Samsung Auto Blocker must not be bypassed. GitHub releases are not Google Play approval.

Outstanding before Play distribution: genuine device screenshots, verified private developer and privacy contact, legal review, platform policy and data-safety review, and an approved Play build/AAB.
